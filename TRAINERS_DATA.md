## Notes & Observations

1. **Standardization:** Most pages (IBC, IBC-Copy, AP, IBDP, IGCSE, MYP, A-Level) share the same 16-trainer roster.

2. **Specialization:** Single-trainer pages focus on specific subjects (e.g., Spanish = AASHIMA, Computer Science = IRTIQA).

3. **Multi-specialty:** Some trainers are featured across multiple specialty pages based on their expertise (e.g., DR. HAYA covers Chemistry, Biology, and Physics).

4. **Home Page:** Uses older placeholder data (NIKHIL PAWAR, PRAKSHI SHARMA) with Lorem ipsum subtitles.

5. **BC Page Variant:** Only page with SADAF trainer; other BC trainers match the standard roster.

6. **Mobile Responsive:** All pages implement Swiper carousel for mobile/tablet views with grid fallback for desktop.

7. **Pagination:** Pages with 10+ trainers include "SEE MORE/SEE LESS" toggle for progressive disclosure.
